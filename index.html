Makery: Pattern Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Makery is a craft pattern companion that processes your patterns and project progress locally on your device. The app does not require an account, collect personal data for the developer, or include advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Patterns and project data

PDFs and images you import, project and pattern names, reading positions, counters, markers, annotations, and preferences are stored locally to provide the app's features. Makery does not upload this content to developer-operated servers or sell or share it with advertisers.

When you import images from Photos, Makery uses Apple's system picker and receives only the images you select, not access to your entire photo library. Importing does not modify your original photos or files.

If you choose a file or photo stored with iCloud or another provider, Apple's system services or that provider may download it for import. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Device backups may also include app data, depending on your device and backup settings. Makery does not operate its own cloud sync or backup service.

Keeping and deleting data

Saved patterns and projects remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app and its data. You can delete projects in the app. Deleting a project does not delete its saved pattern; delete the pattern separately after removing all projects that use it. Deleting imported content in Makery does not delete your original files or photos.

Offloading the app may keep its data. Copies in device backups are managed through your device or backup provider, not by Makery's developer.

Contacting support

If you email makerysupport@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send so that we can respond to your request. This is separate from the app's local processing. Email is handled by email service providers; please send only information needed for your question.

This privacy policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, or embedded third-party resources. GitHub processes website access information, including visitors' IP addresses logged for security purposes, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this website is separate from using the Makery app.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when the app or its privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy. For privacy questions, contact makerysupport@gmail.com.
